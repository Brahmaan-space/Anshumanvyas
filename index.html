About

Profile

I’m a mechanical engineer at D-TA Systems in Ottawa, where I lead structures and integration on EMSO trailer platforms. The job covers the structures themselves, payload integration, power, thermal management and the mechanical interfaces that tie them together.

I completed my master’s in aerospace engineering at Carleton University, where I focused my coursework on orbital mechanics, space control, space robotics and 3D machine vision. My thesis was experimental: multiphase flow through Venturi aspirators, measured with custom 3D-printed geometries and validated against CFD.

The projects on this site are the space side of that background: spacecraft trajectories, a capture manipulator and a LiDAR reconstruction, modelled from first principles in MATLAB.

MASc thesis · 2023–2025

Multiphase flow in Venturi aspirator systems

Designed and 3D printed custom Venturi geometries with integrated instrumentation, then used dimensional analysis to build analytical performance models from single and multiphase air-water experiments.

Validated the experimental findings against CFD in ANSYS Fluent and OpenFOAM across multiple geometric configurations and operating regimes.
